About me

Every day at the bank I sit between customers and the numbers that shape what they can borrow, save and plan. That work taught me how interest rates feel from the ground.

My degree in Mathematics with Economics gave me the models. My Computer Science studies are giving me the tools to test them with data.

My long-term goal is to become a monetary policy economist, contributing to interest-rate and financial-stability decisions at a central bank.

Until then, I’m building a record of clear, data-backed analysis that I can show.
